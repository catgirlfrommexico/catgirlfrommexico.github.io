# on suicide

suicide is always an option, and those that say otherwise are fucking stupid

i want to kill myself. i just don't have the guts to. i have lost everything and the only thing that could help at this point is death. the sweet release of death.

suicide is not "taking the easy way out". in fact, perhaps it should be respected. suicide says "i've taken so much, and this is my final act of mercy against myself. i've fought so hard and so i will be brave enough to help myself and go ahead and end my life". 

but what about the mess one leaves behind?

i don't want my parents to see my dead body. i don't want my friends, pets, family, or anyone i love to see that. i want it to be a quick "this is the end of my life, goodbye.". i'd write notes. i should probably get on it since i do have something of a plan at this point.

the one thing i don't appreciate about suicide, at least in my situation, is that the sheer act of it admits to myself that i couldn't do more. but you can always do more, can't you?

why don't people kill themselves more often? is it fear? spirit? religion? why? 

nevertheless, i perservere, for some reason

but i think, given the opportunity to do it easily and painlessly, i would do it.

but maybe i wouldn't

and i think that's both the beauty and the horror of being human. there's always a chance, no matter how small, to be happy and see happiness in the future

and perhaps its just the way that i'm wired, or perhaps it's the way that everyone is wired. 

but that chance keeps me alive. the knowledge that i could, maybe, just maybe, fix everything. the 0.0000001% chance scenario that i aspire to make possible.

which sounds really stupid when you say it.

anyways, i'd do it if i could

you know that scene from _Manchester by the Sea_? where everything is at ends, and the main character grabs a cops gun and tries to kill himself but fails? i think that's where I'm at.

as a reminder to whatever audience i may or may not have, i do not want to and would never hurt someone in the process of killing myself. i just want to die as quickly as possible without hurting someone else.

fuck cops by the way. they should give me their guns so i can kill myself. 

you know what could be a good strategy? threaten to rob a place and then get shot by cops. it'd be a helluva way to go out.

to get back to the main point though: suicide is strictly the opposite of cowardly, and i think people don't want to kill themselves for other reasons.

i always wondered if i'd the one that got help. the one that got on a TED talk and said "i almost killed myself, here's why you shouldn't"

i think differently though. i don't think like other people, i think. and that makes it harder. i think

suicide does seem like a valid option for me. not one that should strictly be criticized

if there is an afterlife, i hope i get to see my dogs and cats again. i miss you guys.

it's time for me to work now. goodbye for now. maybe we'll meet again. 

and he stood silent
