# re: gender

i think i need to figure out what it means for *me* to be a girl. i.e, why would i be trans in the first place? why would i want to be a girl?

my problem is that i feel like my reasoning for being wanting to be a girl isn't... correct. not that it's not valid, just that it's not correct in the first place

i spent a lot of time thinking about being a girl a month or so ago (realistically i've been thinking about it for months, and considering it for years)

about 2 months ago i wrote that I wanted to start hrt. i don't think i had fully thought everything through at that point though

## example 1

so what does it mean to be a girl to me? let me start with an example: body hair. a lot of people say that if you don't want body hair, that's a sign that you want to be a woman

i certainly don't think that applies to me. i have deduced that my general dislike of body hair is because i want smooth skin.

keyword "i want". not because i experience dysphoria when i see hair on my body. i just don't really like body hair. and i think that's normal (well, normal is speculative, but you get the point)

furthermore, i just like having smooth skin.

but does wanting smooth skin make me want to be a woman? 

this in itself could be interpreted in multiple ways - maybe i just like having smooth skin, maybe i want to be a woman, or maybe i want to be more feminine

## example 2

as for something more holistic, let's consider gender *roles*. these differ from area to area and person to person, but there is certainly a standard. 

women generally have an hourglass figure, not-broad shoulders, and longer hair. men generally have a more straight figure, broad shoulders, and shorter hair.

there are also roles and specifications in the way each gender acts, which i don't feel like i fit into. 

i don't want to be a traditional wife (or maybe I do? i'd have to think abou this more), and i certainly don't want to be the "helpless" girlfriend

i don't want to be "butch" or a tomgirl either

so what gender role does that leave me to fit into?

# back to basics

fundamentally, i keep coming back to the same question: if i could snap my fingers right now and become a girl, would I? this theoretical scenario ensures that i would a) completely pass as a girl b) essentially be "born" as a girl (no one would ever know i had transitioned) and c) i can transition into whatever body i want, aka the perfect transition.

in the past, i would've said yes. but now I'm not so sure. my dysphoria wanes and waxes, and there are days where I don't feel like a girl - i feel like i want to be a big strong man instead.
