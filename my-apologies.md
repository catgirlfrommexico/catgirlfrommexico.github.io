# my apologies

i think i apologise for too much and not enough all at the same time

i've made mistakes, and i've hurt people in unnecessary ways 

i've done that a lot recently, actually

my apologies to those that i've hurt along the way. i've done and said things that i deeply regret, that i will likely never be forgiven for. and i don't know i can move on. not yet, at least.

and yet i will not apologise for what i do not need to apologise for. i do not believe in karma.

if i offend you, you have no right to offend me. if you offend me, i have no right to offend you. it rarely works out that way, but i do believe it's the best and most proper way to operate. 

but how do i move on from those as well? how do i escape what i need not apologise for? i'm not sure yet.

i think it's harder to move on from that than it is to move on from the things that i've done.

with the things that i've done, I know it's my fault, so it's a tiny bit easier to cope. i'm still upset that i did those things but at the very least they were within my control.

when someone else does something that i feel the need to apologise for i think it's a bit harder to cope with that. 

until i figure out a way to cope,

my apologies
