# pros and cons

i can't decide if im trans so i decided to make a list of pros and cons

i also worry that me wanting to do this is a sign that im not trans in itself, because why would a real trans person have to worry about the pros and cons of being trans

but mabye this is one of those "internalized transphobia" things... so i don't know.

anyways

## pros

- more fulfilling relationships, i don't have to feel like i'm hiding anything
- happier with body for a large portion of the time
- happier because i would get called by different name/pronouns
- cute clothes

## cons

- still feel masculine about 1/3 or 1/2 the time
- might not pass
- unable to do inherently masculine things
- potential for brain fog with spiro
- loss of social circle/lack of potential for new friends

# conclusion

i think all of the cons could be ignored if i felt strongly enough about one of the pros. i just don't know if i do

one of the big things that im struggling with is that being trans isn't just "being a cute college girl", it's living as a woman for the rest of my life. that's something that i don't know if i want, which again worries me that i'm not trans.
